Worst-case artwork — same layout, four covers

The rule is structural, not hopeful: no text is ever placed on artwork. The cover is quarantined in a hard-framed, recessed square. Nothing outside that square changes when the cover changes, so there is no cover that can break the card. The only thing the artwork is allowed to touch is the 8px spine and the far background wash — and both are fed a clamped sample.

Full-bleed cover, gradient scrim, white type. It works on the covers you tested with and fails on the ones your players own: on this blown-out white the scrim is the only thing keeping the title alive, and the scrim is a bet. Every genre client makes this bet. We do not take it — the art gets a window, the text gets a plate. That single decision is what constraint 4.1 costs, and it is the cheapest price on the board.
